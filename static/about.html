About Furniture Refurbishment Co.

Your trusted partner for complete furniture restoration solutions in Paços de Ferreira, Portugal.

Based in the heart of Portugal’s renowned furniture hub, Furniture Refurbishment Co. specializes in providing turnkey solutions for furniture restoration. With over a decade of experience, our team of skilled artisans and designers is dedicated to reviving your furniture and transforming spaces with high-quality craftsmanship and personalized service.

We believe that every piece of furniture tells a story, and our mission is to bring those stories back to life with the utmost care and attention to detail. From vintage treasures to modern classics, we handle each project with professionalism, ensuring that your restored furniture not only looks beautiful but is built to last.

Whether you’re a homeowner looking to refresh your living space or a business seeking a complete furniture overhaul, our team is equipped to handle projects of all sizes. Trust us to deliver solutions that exceed expectations and breathe new life into your cherished furniture pieces.
