About Us

Expert Restoration, Maintenance, and New Furniture for Businesses and Homes

At Veldin Solutions, we offer comprehensive services that include expert furniture restorations, corporate maintenance contracts, and bulk orders tailored to meet the needs of businesses and individuals alike. Whether you're looking to restore a cherished piece, maintain your office furniture with ongoing care, or furnish your entire corporate space, we have the expertise and resources to deliver exceptional results.

In addition to restoration and maintenance, we handle bulk orders, providing customized furniture solutions for offices, hotels, restaurants, and other commercial spaces. Whether you need a large order of modern pieces or bespoke, one-of-a-kind items, our team is equipped to handle large-scale projects with efficiency and precision.

Email us with you business needs, and we'll give a free estimate, or build samples that fit your vision!
